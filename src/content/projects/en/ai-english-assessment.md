This was a team project of two, built during my final year at CUHK-SZ as a prototype for the start-up AAIL. The prototype was later integrated into their product, sayo AI.

The tool helps Hong Kong students prepare for the HKDSE English oral exam, specifically the group discussion component, where four candidates hold an English discussion on a designated topic. Our prototype supports real-time video for four participants and, after each session, provides scores and improvement suggestions based on official HKDSE assessment criteria. The real-time video features are powered by third-party APIs.
